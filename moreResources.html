Changing Your Habits for Better Health - National Institute of Diabetes and Digestive and Kidney Diseases

Making healthy lifestyle changes can be challenging, especially with a busy schedule. This NIDDK article explains the four stages of habit change and offers practical strategies for overcoming obstacles, setting realistic goals, planning ahead, and maintaining healthier eating and physical activity routines.

Meal Planning 101 - Mayo Clinic

Meal planning does not have to be overwhelming or time-consuming. This Mayo Clinic Press article provides practical strategies for choosing healthy recipes, preparing meals efficiently, and organizing food for the week, making it easier to maintain nutritious eating habits with a busy schedule.

Eating & Physical Activity to Lose or Maintain Weight - National Institute of Diabetes and Digestive and Kidney Diseases

This NIDDK article explains how a sustainable eating plan and regular physical activity can support weight loss and long-term weight maintenance. It offers guidance on nutritious food choices, recommended activity levels, and practical ways to make healthy habits part of your everyday routine. www.niddk.nih.gov

Building Simple Habits for Healthy Weight Loss - Harvard Health Publishing

This Harvard Health article explains how small, sustainable changes to eating, exercise, sleep, and daily routines can support healthy weight loss. It emphasizes building realistic habits that fit into everyday life rather than relying on quick fixes or restrictive diets.

Balancing healthy meals and busy lives: Associations between work, school and family responsibilities and perceived time constraints among young adults - National Library of Medicine

This research article examines how work, school, and family responsibilities can create time constraints that make healthy eating and meal preparation more difficult. It highlights the challenges busy young adults face when trying to balance nutritious meals with competing demands and limited time.
